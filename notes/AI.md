#  Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broad field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. These tasks include reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, and decision-making.

## How It Works

Traditional AI relied on **rule-based systems (expert systems)**, where developers manually defined a set of rules for solving problems. Modern AI combines these approaches with data-driven techniques, allowing systems to learn from data and improve over time.

## Key Characteristics

- Simulates human intelligence
- Solves complex problems
- Learns from data
- Automates decision-making
- Performs repetitive tasks efficiently

## Applications

- Virtual assistants
- Autonomous vehicles
- Healthcare
- Robotics
- Finance
- Cybersecurity

## Importance

AI serves as the foundation of modern intelligent systems and enables automation across nearly every industry.
