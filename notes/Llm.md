# Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced deep learning models trained on massive amounts of text data to understand and generate human language.

Most modern LLMs are built using the **Transformer architecture**, which allows them to capture long-range relationships between words and sentences.

## How It Works

LLMs predict the next token (word or subword) in a sequence based on the context provided. Through training on billions of examples, they learn grammar, reasoning patterns, facts, programming languages, and general knowledge.

## Capabilities

- Text generation
- Question answering
- Code generation
- Translation
- Summarization
- Content creation

## Examples

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Claude
- Llama
- Mistral

## Importance

LLMs have transformed how people interact with AI by enabling natural conversations and solving a wide variety of language-related tasks.
